# Privacy Policy

This policy applies to superloom.dev and Superloom apps and services that link to this page.

## Information we use

When you use Superloom, we may receive basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser or device type, and request details. If you contact us, we receive the contact details and message you provide. An app may also use information you provide or authorize to deliver a feature you choose to use.

We use this information to provide and secure Superloom, troubleshoot problems, and respond to requests. Service providers may process information to help us do this. We may also disclose information when required by law. We keep it for as long as needed for these purposes or as required by law.

## Questions and requests

For privacy questions or requests to access, correct, or delete your information, use the [AI Wonderland contact form](https://aiwonderland.co/contact). If an app uses additional information, check its own privacy notice for details.
